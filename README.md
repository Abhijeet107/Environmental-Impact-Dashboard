## Project Objective
. Developed a dashboard to monitor environmental metrics across industries, regions, and compliance levels to identify areas for improvement.
## Dataset Used
. Data included metrics on carbon emissions, recycling rates, energy usage, and water consumption categorized by region, industry, and compliance.
## KPIs
. Total Carbon Emissions: 16.2M kg
. Average Recycling Rate: 72.02%
. Total Energy Usage: 37.5M kWh
. Total Water Consumption: 49.9M liters
## Process
. Consolidated and cleaned regional, industrial, and compliance data.
. Used Tableau to create interactive visualizations such as bar charts, pie charts, and trend lines.
. Enabled filtering by year, product, industry, region, and compliance levels for dynamic analysis.
## Project Insights
. Regional Analysis: Asia shows the highest total carbon emissions (23.5M kg).
. Recycling Rates: Industries like Technology and Automotive have higher recycling rates across most regions.
. Compliance Levels: Distribution of compliance is nearly uniform at 33% for low, medium, and high levels.
. Trends in Energy Usage: Significant spikes and declines observed, highlighting potential inefficiencies.
## Conclusion
. The dashboard provides actionable insights to improve recycling rates and reduce carbon emissions by identifying non-compliant regions and industries.
. Supports sustainability efforts by highlighting areas to optimize energy and water usage.
. Aids stakeholders in making data-driven environmental strategies.
